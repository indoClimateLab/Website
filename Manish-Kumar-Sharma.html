  2. Manish Kumar Sharma

Dr. Manish Kumar is an Assistant Professor at Amity University, Noida, specializing in Environmental Sciences, with a focus on CO₂ sequestration, biofuels, biomaterials, and resource recovery. His research has garnered significant recognition, placing him among the top 2% of highly cited scientists globally.

Dr. Kumar holds a PhD in Environmental Sciences from Jawaharlal Nehru University (JNU), New Delhi, India. Thereafter, he pursued his postdoctoral research at The Hong Kong Polytechnic University (PolyU), Hong Kong SAR. His work primarily addresses pressing environmental challenges, particularly the mitigation of climate change through effective CO₂ sequestration techniques. He has extensively explored the development of sustainable biofuels and biomaterials, contributing to advancements in renewable energy sources and eco-friendly materials. His research also delves into resource recovery, aiming to optimize the utilization of natural resources and minimize environmental impact.

Throughout his academic career, Dr. Kumar has published numerous papers that have collectively been cited over 9,000 times, reflecting the substantial impact of his contributions to environmental science. His work not only advances scientific understanding but also offers practical solutions to environmental issues, underscoring his commitment to sustainability and innovation.

In addition to his research, Dr. Kumar is dedicated to education and mentorship, guiding students in environmental science and fostering the next generation of researchers. His role at Amity University involves both teaching and leading research initiatives, where he continues to explore innovative approaches to environmental sustainability. Dr. Kumar's expertise and contributions have established him as a leading figure in environmental science, with his research playing a crucial role in addressing global environmental challenges and promoting sustainable development.
